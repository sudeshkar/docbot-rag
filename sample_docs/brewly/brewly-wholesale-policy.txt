BREWLY WHOLESALE PROGRAM

Who can apply
Cafes, restaurants, offices and grocery stores in the United States can apply for a wholesale account. Wholesale is not yet available in Canada.

Minimum order
The minimum wholesale order is 5 kg per delivery. Orders above 20 kg per month get a 15% volume discount.

Pricing and payment
Wholesale prices are 30% below retail. New accounts pay by card for the first three orders. After that, approved accounts can pay by invoice with net-30 terms.

Equipment and training
Wholesale customers who order at least 10 kg per month get one free barista training session per quarter at their location, within 50 miles of our roastery in Portland, Oregon. We do not sell or lease espresso machines.

How to apply
Fill in the form at brewly.example/wholesale. Applications are reviewed within 3 business days. Questions can be sent to wholesale@brewly.example.
