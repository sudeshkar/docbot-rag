# Shipping and Returns

## Where we ship

Brewly ships to the United States and Canada. We do not ship to PO boxes in Canada. International shipping outside these two countries is not available yet.

## Delivery times

We roast on Monday and ship on Tuesday. Delivery takes 2 to 4 business days in the United States and 4 to 8 business days in Canada. You will get a tracking link by email as soon as your order ships.

## Canadian customers

Canadian orders are shipped duties-paid, so you will not be charged extra at the door. Prices are shown in US dollars and converted by your bank.

## Damaged or missing orders

If your bag arrives damaged, or tracking shows delivered but you did not receive it, contact us within 14 days of the delivery date. Send a photo of the damaged package if possible. We will send a free replacement with the next roast; we do not ask you to return damaged coffee.

## Returns and refunds

Because coffee is a food product, we cannot accept returns of opened bags. If you do not like a coffee, tell us within 30 days and we will give you a credit for one bag on your next delivery, once per customer per year.

Unopened bags can be returned within 30 days for a full refund. The customer pays return shipping. Refunds go back to the original payment method within 5 business days of us receiving the bag.

## Contact

Email hello@brewly.example or use the chat on our website. Our team answers Monday to Friday, 9 am to 5 pm Eastern Time, usually within 4 hours.
