# Brewly Help Center: FAQ

Brewly is a coffee subscription service. We roast specialty beans every Monday and ship them fresh to subscribers.

## Subscriptions

### What plans do you offer?

Brewly has three plans:

- **Starter:** one 250 g bag every 4 weeks for $14 per delivery.
- **Regular:** two 250 g bags every 2 weeks for $26 per delivery.
- **Office:** a 1 kg bag every week for $48 per delivery, with an invoice option for companies.

Shipping is free on the Regular and Office plans. Starter deliveries cost $4.50 to ship.

### How do I pause or skip a delivery?

Go to My Account > Subscription > Upcoming deliveries and click "Skip" next to any delivery. You can pause your subscription for up to 3 months with "Pause subscription". Changes must be made before 11:59 pm (Eastern Time) on the Thursday before the roast date, because we lock orders on Friday.

### How do I cancel?

You can cancel any time from My Account > Subscription > Cancel subscription. There is no cancellation fee. If you cancel after the Friday order lock, the next delivery will still ship and be charged.

### Can I change my grind?

Yes. Choose whole bean, espresso, filter, French press or cold brew grind under My Account > Preferences. The change applies to the next delivery that has not been locked yet.

## Coffee

### Where do your beans come from?

We buy directly from farms in Ethiopia, Colombia, Guatemala and Kenya. Each bag lists the farm, altitude, variety and processing method on the label.

### How should I store my coffee?

Keep beans in the resealable bag or an airtight container, away from light and heat. Do not store coffee in the fridge. Whole beans taste best within 4 weeks of the roast date; ground coffee within 2 weeks.

### Do you offer decaf?

Yes. Our decaf is a Colombian coffee decaffeinated with the Swiss Water process, which uses no chemical solvents. Select "Decaf" under My Account > Preferences.

## Account

### I forgot my password

Click "Forgot password" on the login page. The reset link expires after 2 hours.

### Gift subscriptions

Gift subscriptions are prepaid for 3, 6 or 12 months and do not renew automatically. The recipient gets an email with a redemption code that is valid for one year.
